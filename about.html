About Me

Hi, I’m Yuhan.

I also go by Yvonne. I’m pursuing a Master of Industrial Design at Georgia Tech. I studied industrial design at Pratt Institute, where I also studied psychology and UI/UX design.

I’m curious about how physical objects shape the way we interact with technology. My work moves between research, sketching, and hands-on prototyping, from musical instruments to products for everyday routines.
